Our Programs

Four Pillars of
Community Strength

Every program we run is built around the lived experiences of Somali American families — designed with community, not just for community.

Our Approach

Culturally grounded programs that create real change

At SAP, we don't believe in one-size-fits-all solutions. Every program is developed in deep partnership with Somali community members — reflecting their language, values, and lived realities.

From health navigation to workforce development, our four pillars work together to support the whole family across every stage of life in Minnesota.

01
Health
Culturally Responsive Health Navigation

Many Somali families face significant barriers to healthcare — language, cultural differences, and complex systems. SAP bridges that gap with trusted, culturally informed health navigators who speak your language and understand your community.

  • Health insurance enrollment and benefits navigation
  • Mental health support and culturally sensitive counseling referrals
  • Maternal and child health resources
  • Chronic disease management education
  • Connection to Somali-speaking healthcare providers
300+
Families connected to healthcare

Our health navigators are members of the Somali community — trusted guides who meet families where they are.

02
Education
Youth Mentorship & Academic Success

Somali American youth navigate a dual identity — honoring their heritage while building their future in America. SAP provides the mentorship, academic support, and safe spaces they need to thrive in both worlds.

  • One-on-one mentorship with Somali American role models
  • After-school academic tutoring and homework support
  • Youth leadership development programs
  • College access and financial aid guidance
  • Youth substance use prevention programs
1,500+
Youth served through education programs

SAP's youth programs are funded in part by a Minneapolis City youth substance use prevention grant.

03
Financial Stability
Economic Empowerment & Workforce Development

Building generational wealth starts with access — to jobs, financial tools, and economic opportunity. SAP's workforce development programs are backed by a Minneapolis City Council contract and designed to open real doors for Somali American adults.

  • Job readiness training and resume support
  • Financial literacy and banking access workshops
  • Small business development resources
  • Employer partnership and job placement
  • Benefits navigation and public assistance guidance
2026–28
Minneapolis City Council workforce contract

SAP was authorized by Minneapolis City Council to deliver workforce development services across the Twin Cities.

04
Community Safety
Community Safety Net & Emergency Support

When families face crisis — housing instability, food insecurity, or emergency needs — SAP is there. Our community safety net programs ensure no Somali family in Minnesota faces hardship alone.

  • Emergency food and basic needs assistance
  • Housing stability and eviction prevention resources
  • Community advocacy and rights education
  • Safety planning and domestic violence resources
  • Connection to legal aid and immigration support
450+
Families supported through safety net

Our safety net programs operate year-round, with emergency response capacity for families in immediate crisis.
