About Us

Who We Are &
Why We Exist

Somali American Partnerships was founded to fill a critical gap — a trusted, culturally grounded organization that Somali families in the Twin Cities can turn to at every stage of their journey.

Our Mission

Empowering families through culture, partnership, and community

We connect Somali American families in Minneapolis with culturally responsive programs across health, education, financial stability, and community safety — building bridges between our community and the resources they deserve.

Our Story

From a vision to a growing organization

Somali American Partnerships was born out of a simple but urgent observation — that despite being one of the largest Somali diaspora communities in the United States, Minneapolis lacked a dedicated, culturally grounded nonprofit focused on the full spectrum of Somali American family needs.

Founded in December 2024, SAP was built to change that. We started with a clear mandate: create programs that honor Somali culture while building real, lasting bridges to opportunity in Minnesota.

In our first year, we secured a Minneapolis City Council workforce development contract, launched youth prevention programs, and began building the institutional partnerships that will fuel our growth for years to come.
