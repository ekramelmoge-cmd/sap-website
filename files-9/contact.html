Institutional Partners

Schools, healthcare systems, city agencies, and corporations can partner with SAP to co-deliver programs, share resources, and expand our collective reach across the Twin Cities.

Funders & Grantmakers

SAP welcomes grant funding, corporate giving, and philanthropic partnerships. We provide detailed impact reporting and full financial transparency to all funders.
