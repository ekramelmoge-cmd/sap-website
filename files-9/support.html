For You

Whatever you're facing — health challenges, financial hardship, housing instability, or youth support — SAP is your trusted partner. All services are free, confidential, and culturally responsive.

Our Services

How We Can Help

Health Support

Connecting you to healthcare, mental health support, and wellness resources in a culturally safe environment.

  • Health insurance enrollment help
  • Finding Somali-speaking doctors
  • Mental health referrals
  • Maternal and child health
  • Chronic disease management
Request Health Support
Youth & Education

Academic support, mentorship, and youth development programs for Somali American children and young adults.

  • After-school tutoring
  • Youth mentorship matching
  • College access guidance
  • Youth leadership programs
  • Substance use prevention
Enroll Your Child
Financial & Employment

Job readiness, financial literacy, and economic empowerment resources to build your family's financial future.

  • Resume and job application help
  • Financial literacy workshops
  • Banking and credit guidance
  • Small business support
  • Benefits and public assistance navigation
Get Financial Help
Emergency & Safety

When you need immediate help — food, housing, safety — SAP's community safety net is here for you.

  • Emergency food assistance
  • Housing stability support
  • Domestic safety resources
  • Legal aid referrals
  • Immigration support connections

Our Process

How to Get Started

1
Reach Out

Call us, fill out our form, or walk into our office. No appointment needed for urgent needs.

2
We Listen

A culturally responsive SAP team member will listen to your needs with care and without judgment.

3
We Connect You

We match you with the right programs, resources, and community partners for your situation.

4
We Walk With You

SAP stays with you through the process — we don't just refer, we support.
