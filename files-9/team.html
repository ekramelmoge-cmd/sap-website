Our Team

Dedicated professionals with deep roots in both the Somali American community and Minnesota's nonprofit and civic landscape.

Organizational Leadership

Our Team

Ekram Elmoge
Executive Director

Provides overall organizational leadership and strategic direction for SAP. Oversees all programs, partnerships, fundraising, and operations to advance SAP's mission of serving Somali American families across the Twin Cities.

Misbil Haji
Health Department Director

Oversees SAP's health programs, including culturally responsive health navigation, insurance enrollment support, maternal and child health resources, and connections to Somali-speaking healthcare providers across the Twin Cities.

Amin Abdullahi
Director of Programs

Directs SAP's full program portfolio including youth mentorship, education support, financial literacy, workforce development, and community safety initiatives. Ensures programs are culturally grounded and delivering measurable impact for families.

Sudais Oumar
Administrative Support

Supports the day-to-day administrative operations of SAP, ensuring smooth coordination across teams, programs, and community partners to keep the organization running effectively.
