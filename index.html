Who We Are

A 501(c)(3) rooted in dignity, culture, and belonging

Founded in December 2024, Somali American Partnerships was built to fill a critical gap — community-centered programs that honor Somali culture while building lasting bridges in Minnesota.

We believe every Somali American family deserves access to the resources, support, and opportunities they need to thrive — in their own language, on their own terms.

What We Do

Four Pillars of Community Strength

Every program is built around the lived experiences of Somali American families — designed with community, not just for community.

01
Health
Culturally Responsive Health Navigation

Bridging Somali families to accessible, culturally informed healthcare and wellness resources across the Twin Cities.
02
Education
Youth Mentorship & Academic Success

Mentorship and academic support helping youth navigate their dual identity and achieve educational success.
03
Financial Stability
Economic Empowerment & Workforce Development

Workforce development and financial literacy initiatives that build generational wealth for families.
04
Community Safety
Community Safety Net & Emergency Support

Emergency support and community advocacy ensuring no Somali family in Minnesota faces hardship alone.
Our Impact

Making a real difference in the Twin Cities
